# Player Guide

## Placing a display

1. Stand in your trophy room and press **F7** (or `/trophyroom`).
2. Pick **Mannequin** (or **Weapon**):
   - Mannequin: choose gender, outfit source (*My current outfit*, *Bare mannequin*,
     or a saved outfit where supported), pose, and stand.
   - Weapon: choose wall mount / floor stand / glass case and type the weapon item
     name. Your actual weapon (with its serial, tint and attachments) is taken from
     your inventory when you confirm — retrieving the display returns exactly it.
3. **Placement controls**: Arrow keys move · PgUp/PgDn height · Q/E rotate ·
   Shift = fine · G = snap to ground · Tab = grid snap · X = collision preview ·
   **Enter confirm** · **Backspace cancel**.

## Interacting with displays

Look at a display and use your target (or press E):

- **Inspect** — details, rarity, achievement info; rename if it's yours.
- **Try outfit** — temporarily wear a mannequin's outfit. Your own clothes come back
  automatically (timer, Backspace, or if anything interrupts).
- **Change outfit** (owner) — the mannequin copies what you are wearing right now.
- **Change pose / Move / Remove** (owner).
- **Retrieve weapon** (owner) — the weapon returns to your inventory.

## Good to know

- Mannequins never show skin. If an outfit contains a garment the system can't render
  safely yet, the mannequin stays in clean plastic and tells you why — ask an admin to
  run the conversion pipeline for that garment.
- Displays are per-property: visitors in the same property instance see exactly what
  you see; other instances don't.
- There are limits per room and per player — the UI tells you when you hit them.
